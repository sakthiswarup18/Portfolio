The three dashboards on the home page are not screenshots, and they are not embedded Tableau or Power BI reports. Each chart is plain SVG drawn by a few hundred lines of JavaScript.

## Why no charting library

A library would add weight to draw a handful of charts that each need something specific. Writing the scales, tick choices and path strings myself kept the page light, and it is the part of a portfolio piece worth showing.

The code is split in two. One half is arithmetic: scales, medians, histograms. It touches no page elements, so it can be tested on its own. The other half turns those numbers into shapes.

## Why the data is synthetic

Three years of real healthcare data is not mine to publish. Every number you see is generated by seeded random functions, so the dashboards look the same on every visit and nobody's record is in them.

## What I would do with real tools

At work the same ideas go into Tableau and Power BI: keep heavy calculation in SQL and Python, keep the reporting layer fast, and make the first screen readable in thirty seconds.
