Approach

How I work

Step 01

Understand the problem before the data

Business context first. What decision does this model serve? Who acts on it? What does a wrong prediction cost? I map those stakes before writing a single line of code.

Step 02

Iterate fast, fail at low cost

A simple baseline beats a sophisticated model that ships late. I build the simplest thing that could work, measure it honestly, and layer complexity only where it earns its keep.

Step 03

Make results legible to non-specialists

A model nobody trusts doesn't get used. I invest in explainability, clean visualizations, and plain-language summaries so insights land with everyone who needs them.
